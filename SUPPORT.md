# Support Guide

Welcome to the BioSphere Support Center! We are dedicated to providing excellent developer and learner experience. Please refer to the resources below to get help or answer questions.

---

## 🔍 Step 1: Browse Existing Resources

Before asking a question or filing a ticket, please review the following:

1. **The README FAQ:** Check the [Troubleshooting & FAQ](#-faq--troubleshooting) section of the root `README.md` file.
2. **GitHub Discussions:** Browse topics, questions, and community answers.
3. **Open & Closed Issues:** Search for error codes or keywords to find previous solutions.

---

## 💬 Step 2: Get Community Support

### Ask a Question
If you have a general question about how to set up simulations, configure Supabase, or understand 3D components:
- Submit a **Question** issue template on our [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/your-username/biosphere/issues) board.
- Post a question in the community Discussion forum.

### Discuss Features
Have an amazing idea for a new virtual lab or an ecosystem component?
- Open a discussion thread under the **Ideas** or **Features** category.

---

## 🛡️ Step 3: Enterprise & Private Support

If your inquiry relates to:
- Reporting security vulnerabilities (refer to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md))
- Partnering or institutional integrations
- Educational license inquiries

Please contact us directly via email at: **`support@biosphere.example.com`**
